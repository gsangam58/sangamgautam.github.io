05 / RESEARCHResearch

Landslides, models & spatial decision-making.

My undergraduate research applied Frequency Ratio, Logistic Regression and Random Forest models to landslide susceptibility mapping along the Butwal–Tansen section of the Siddhartha Highway.

The workflow included feature-importance and multicollinearity analysis using Information Gain, Pearson Correlation, VIF and Tolerance, followed by model evaluation using AUC-ROC, accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.
